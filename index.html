suginami-town-api

Open data about the neighborhoods of Suginami, Tokyo, as static JSON. 杉並区のまちのオープンデータ。

  • v0/index.json: the 23 neighborhood areas, with a path to each one
  • v0/areas/{id}.json: one area in full, e.g. Kugayama
  • v0/spots.json: every place worth visiting
  • v0/happenings.json: every dated happening, earliest first
  • v0/kami.json: the kami enshrined at Suginami's shrines
  • v0/content.json: all of it in one file
  • v0/map.en.kml and v0/map.ja.kml: import into Google My Maps, Google Earth or Organic Maps (how)
  • v0/spots.geojson: every place, for developers
  • v0/towns.geojson: the 139 towns (丁目) as polygons, with official census codes

v0 is not yet stable. Details, versioning and attribution are in the README.

Town boundaries: 「令和2年国勢調査町丁・字等別境界データ」（総務省統計局）を加工して作成。出典：政府統計の総合窓口(e-Stat)
