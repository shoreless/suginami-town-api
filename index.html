suginami-town-api

Open data about the neighborhoods of Suginami, Tokyo, as static JSON. 杉並区のまちのオープンデータ。

  • v0/content.json: the 23 neighborhood areas, their towns, places and happenings, in English and Japanese
  • v0/towns.geojson: the 139 towns (丁目) as polygons, with official census codes

v0 is not yet stable. Details, versioning and attribution are in the README.

Town boundaries: 「令和2年国勢調査町丁・字等別境界データ」（総務省統計局）を加工して作成。出典：政府統計の総合窓口(e-Stat)
